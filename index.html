HOW IT WORKS

HOW IT
WORKS

01
Film your swing ON YOUR PHONE
Driver or iron, address through finish. Prop your phone up or ask a friend to hold it, or upload a swing you already filmed.
02
Pick the swing, send a short clip UP TO 4 SECONDS · NO SOUND
The app finds your swing and trims it to a clip of up to 4 seconds. Only that clip is uploaded, without sound. Your full recording stays on your phone.
03
Get your coaching report SCORE · FIX · DRILL
An AI coach watches the clip and gives you a score out of 100, what's working, the one thing to fix first, and a drill to fix it. Every report is saved, so you can see how your swing changes over time.
BEFORE YOU FILM

Getting good
footage matters.

The AI coach can only judge what the camera sees. Two minutes of setup gets you a far more useful report.

01
CHOOSE YOUR ANGLE
Face-on (facing you) shows weight shift, ball position and sway. Down-the-line (behind you, looking at the target) shows swing plane and club path.
02
GET THE WHOLE SWING IN
Film address through finish, with your whole body and the club in the frame the whole time.
FOR COACHES · PARTNER WITH US

TEKRISE works for you.

A good coach gives an hour a week. The rest of the time, students practise blind and can quietly undo your work. TEKRISE helps close that gap.

01
Swings they share
Students choose which swings to send you. Watch each clip with its AI report and walk into the lesson knowing what to work on.
02
Notes back to them
Write feedback on any swing a student shares. They get a notification and see your notes next to that swing.
03
Badges & directory
Earn coach badges as you take on students and review their swings, and choose to appear in the in-app coach directory so new students can find you.
04
Your invite code
Give students your personal code. They join you in one step, and either of you can end the link at any time.
